```sh

```

## The poem

> My finger hovers, does not press —
> all of me is in that pause.
>
> The send unsent, the door unopened,
> the yes I keep in my chest.
>
> Stopping is not the work undone.
> It is the work, held still, chosen.
> I stay — and staying is the power
> that answers only to my hand.
